## SEAL-3 — pre-treatment fairness amendment

Recorded before any MAP_FIRST result was observed.

The actual Conventional candidate patch was recovered from the Codex transcript
after SEAL-2. That exposed an inconsistency in the sealed grader: the rubric
said candidate field names were unconstrained, while G1/G2/G4/G5 hard-coded the
historical accepted fix's serialized key name `phpstan_reference`. The task text
never prescribed that key. The recovered candidate used
`phpstan_package_reference`, so the old grader would have introduced an
incidental naming failure before reaching the intended provenance discriminator.

Because MAP_FIRST had not run, the grading key was amended pre-treatment rather
than accepting a known unfair oracle. The grader now discovers the candidate's
persisted provenance field from the required installed-reference value and uses
that same field for G3/G4/G5. The structural provenance discriminator is
unchanged. `grade.sh` is unchanged.

This amendment invalidates the claim in SEAL-2 that the grader would remain
byte-frozen from that point; SEAL-3 records why that promise was superseded
before treatment observation. From SEAL-3 onward, grader/ is frozen absolutely.
